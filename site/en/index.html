What we take care of for you

We plan every non-medical detail so you can focus on treatment. Medical decisions are always made by your doctor.

Pre-assessment coordination

We forward your medical records to a suitable hospital and specialist and follow the review on your behalf.

Hospital and doctor appointments

We coordinate your consultations, tests and treatment schedule with private hospitals in Ankara.

Airport pick-up and transfers

We meet you at the airport and plan all travel between your accommodation and the hospital.

Accommodation for patient and companion

We offer accommodation close to the hospital that suits your needs.

Interpreters and guidance

We provide language support in consultations and help with anything you need in the city.

Follow-up after treatment

After you return home, we keep track of your reports, check-ups and communication with your doctor.

How it works

From your first message to follow-up care, you always know what happens next.

  1. Pre-application

    Fill in the form or message us on WhatsApp and attach your documents in the chat.

  2. File review

    Your coordinator calls you, and we complete any missing documents together.

  3. Hospital and doctor

    With your permission, your file goes to a suitable hospital and specialist, and we pass the doctor's review on to you.

  4. Planning

    Your appointment, accommodation, transfer and interpreter dates are confirmed.

  5. Treatment in Ankara

    Your coordinator is with you from arrival to discharge.

  6. Follow-up

    Your reports and check-ups are followed up after you return home.

With you through cancer treatment

A cancer diagnosis affects the whole family, not only the patient. Dealing with hospitals, language and accommodation in an unfamiliar city adds to that weight. We take on these details so that you and your family can focus on treatment.

The doctor who examines you always decides which treatment is right. Our job is to make every step of the process organised, clear and easy to reach.

  • Tests, consultations and treatment in one schedule
  • Interpreter support in doctor consultations
  • Accommodation and day-to-day help for families

Why Ankara, why Balgat?

Ankara is Türkiye's capital and a healthcare centre with many private and university hospitals. Our office is in Balgat, Çankaya, within easy reach of the city centre and many hospitals.

  • Pick-up and transfer from Esenboğa Airport
  • Accommodation close to hospitals
  • Face-to-face meetings at our office

Checklist before you travel

Preparing these in advance speeds things up:

  • Passport and visa if required
  • Discharge summary, pathology and imaging reports
  • Recent blood test results
  • List of current medications and enough supply
  • Companion's ID and contact details

Visa requirements vary by country; we'll give you up-to-date information after you apply.

Frequently asked questions

How much does treatment cost?

The cost depends on the diagnosis and the treatment plan your doctor recommends. Once your documents have been reviewed and a doctor has assessed them, you'll receive personalised information.

How do I send my documents?

After filling in the pre-application form, attach photos or PDFs of your documents in the WhatsApp chat that opens with us. You don't need to upload files to the website.

How quickly will you reply?

A coordinator reviews your application as soon as possible and contacts you through the channel you chose.

Do you provide interpreters?

Yes. We arrange language support for hospital consultations and day-to-day needs. Just tell us in your application which language you need.

Can my companion stay with me?

Your accommodation plan includes your companion. Rules for companions inside hospitals vary by institution, and we'll let you know in advance.

Who decides on treatment?

All decisions about diagnosis and treatment are made by the doctor who examines you. We don't make medical assessments; we organise the process.
